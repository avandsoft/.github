# AVANDSOFT — "SEMICOLON: THE PAUSE THAT DECIDES"
### A Video Scenario for Google Vids
**Director's Cut — Product Launch Film**
**Runtime: 75 seconds | Format: 16:9, 1080p | Tone: Cinematic, confident, human**

---

## DIRECTOR'S NOTE

Every great decision film has one thing in common: a beat of hesitation before clarity. That's our whole story. We open on the noise of indecision — quick cuts, cluttered visuals, a racing pulse — and we resolve it into calm, structured light. The product isn't a gadget, it's a *mind*. We shoot it like one: clean geometry, deliberate pacing, a punctuation mark as our visual motif. The semicolon — a pause, not a stop — becomes the film's recurring symbol, appearing as a light trail, a transition wipe, and finally as the literal logo reveal.

Color palette: deep navy and charcoal base, with a single accent color (electric cyan or amber) representing "clarity" cutting through desaturated "noise" scenes.

---

## SCENE 1 — "THE NOISE" (0:00–0:08)
**Visual:** Fast-cut montage, handheld/shaky energy. A person at a kitchen table surrounded by scattered papers, a phone showing stock charts, a laptop with too many browser tabs. Quick flashes: a spinning "loading" wheel, a crumpled sticky note that says "BUY?", a clock ticking.
**Camera:** Whip pans, tight close-ups, slight Dutch angle to convey unease.
**Color grade:** Desaturated, cool gray, slightly overexposed.
**Sound design:** Overlapping notification pings, a low anxious hum, muffled voices.
**On-screen text:** *(none — let the chaos speak)*
**VO (calm, low, almost a whisper):** "Every day, we face choices that deserve better than a guess."

## SCENE 2 — "THE PAUSE" (0:08–0:14)
**Visual:** Hard cut to black. A single point of cyan light appears center-frame and slowly draws itself into a semicolon ( ; ) — the dot settling first, then the comma-tail extending like a brushstroke of light.
**Camera:** Static, locked-off, slow push-in.
**Color grade:** Pure black background, one glowing accent color.
**Sound design:** The noise cuts to silence, then a single soft chime as the semicolon completes.
**On-screen text:** **SEMICOLON** fades in beneath the mark.
**VO:** "This is Semicolon — the pause before a smarter decision."

**Color grade:** Cool navy UI with cyan highlights on key data points.
**Sound design:** Light, precise UI tick sounds; a subtle rising synth pad underneath.
**On-screen text (animated, left-aligned):**

## PRODUCTION NOTES FOR GOOGLE VIDS

- **Pacing:** Scenes 1–2 should feel fast then suddenly still — this contrast is the emotional hinge of the film. Use Google Vids' transition/timing controls to keep Scene 1 under 8 seconds and Scene 2 held on stillness.
- **Recurring motif:** Use the same semicolon graphic/transition asset as a wipe between Scenes 3→4 and 4→5 for visual continuity; Google Vids' built-in transition library can approximate this with a "light sweep" or "glow wipe" transition if a custom asset isn't available.
- **Voiceover:** One consistent VO artist throughout — calm, mid-register, minimal inflection until the final line, which should land slightly warmer/more human.
- **Music:** A single evolving score cue (not scene-by-scene stock tracks) that shifts from tense/ambient (Scene 1) → minimal/precise (Scenes 2–3) → confident/rising (Scenes 4–6) → warm/resolved (Scenes 7–8). If Google Vids' stock library is used, choose one track and let its built-in energy curve carry the arc rather than switching tracks per scene.
- **Typography:** Use a single clean sans-serif throughout (e.g., a geometric grotesk) for all on-screen text, weight varying only for emphasis — reinforces the "structured, not chaotic" brand promise.
- **Alt shorter cut:** For a 30-second social version, use Scenes 1, 2, 6 (trimmed), 7, and 8 only.
